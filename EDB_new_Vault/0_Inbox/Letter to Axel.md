At Lake Garda with Connie and H. Obviously - you could not go to Lake Konstanz. The potential for confusion is worthy of a Gedicht des Monats. 

Fifty-feckin-two. Ugh. I feel every ounce of it, mostly in my over-trodden feet. Imagine I'd been a smoker all this time. They must feel rancid, not just creaky. Where is all the German spliff that was supposed to happen and blow across the Polish-German border?

Du hast mich irgendwie inspiriert mein locales Goethe Institut zu besuchen um Grammatikbücher auszuleihen. ich höre nicht auf! Als ich sterbe, werde ich noch rätseln "Ich beeile mich? My German is like a Linux distro that needs to be constantly tweaked. There's always a driver that is a bit better than what was in the previous kernel, but it's not quite 100%.

Von meinem deutsch-medien-lesse habe erwissen, dass ein häufiges Thema ist: "Röbert Habeck hat's gesagt." Energie, Rußland, wer weiss mehr?
