#writing
the abrasions of daily life cause nicks in the soul and they build up. Pleasant moments count too, but they don't heal the scars. Over time, what happens to the scars? They build up and make it difficult to move. Your thinking atrophies, so that all you think about are the scars. Something could loosen them and give you back some agility. When you are frozen, a joule of energy makes a difference.
