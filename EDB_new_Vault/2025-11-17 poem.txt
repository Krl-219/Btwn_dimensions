#poem
Get the message

Out here 
in Europe's borderlands
Time flies past.
How to fill it well?

But there's no point pretending,
If you wrote to me -
I may have missed it

Is it two years or five years
To become just a Facebook face?
Connection over here
Would mean disconnection there

It is time to get the message
Rather than linger like a ghost 
Social media is a circle of Dante's hell

Inadvertently he showed me how not to live life
Sniping from the side
"Valuing my independence"

What if what's around is not worth what it pretends?
So much is ostentation, not connection.
