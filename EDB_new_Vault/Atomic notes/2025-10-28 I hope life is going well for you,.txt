I hope life is going well for you, Andrew and Olivia, and especially Catherine -at least calmer after what Patrick has put you through.

A and I feel for your mother’s situation. He pulled the rug from under the whole family, but especially her.

I have rarely had much contact with Patrick, but that aside, he was being secretive with all but a few, to put it at its simplest. 

By the way, when I write “you” below, I mean the three of you collectively, Olivia and Andrew too. 

I am getting in touch because of Mum and Dad, Pat and Pauline. They were here in Kraków for a visit recently for Aleksandra’s exhibition opening. I only get to see them once or twice a year, so the effects of time and age are becoming more marked whenever we meet. It dawned on me that they will probably not be up to another visit. That was the last time. It will be too much for them, especially Dad. That’s why I am getting in touch. Before too long -though there’s nothing immediately urgent to worry about- they will not be there. I never knew my grandfathers, so there was never a chance to hear about life from them. Who knows? Maybe I would not have liked what they had to say, but Aleksandra still appreciates a conversation with her grandmother, who is 97. 

My parents are not emotionally demonstrative, but they do care about their connection with their grandchildren, deep down. They feel terrible about Patrick’s betrayal, at least that’s my interpretation of what he has done, but they were as unwitting as most of us.

It would mean so much to them if you could make contact with them before it’s too late, with all consideration for your loyalty to Catherine. A text or WhatsApp to say how you are doing would be a gift for them.

With my best wishes to all of you,

Evan, on behalf of Andrew and Keith too.
