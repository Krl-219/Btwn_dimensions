Role               | Single Line Description                                                                 |
|--------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Publisher/CEO**  | Oversees the entire organization, sets strategic direction, and ensures financial viability. |
| **Editor-in-Chief**| Leads editorial vision, content quality, and overall publication standards.             |
| **Managing Editor**| Manages daily operations, workflow, and coordinates between departments.               |
| **Section Editors**| Heads specific sections (e.g., news, features, sports), assigns stories, and edits content. |
| **Senior Editors** | Provides high-level editing, mentors writers, and ensures content aligns with editorial standards. |
| **Sub-Editors**    | Focuses on copy editing, fact-checking, and preparing content for publication.          |
| **Writers**        | Researches and creates original content, articles, or reports.                          |
| **Proofreaders**   | Reviews final drafts for grammar, spelling, punctuation, and consistency.               |
| **Production**     | Handles layout, design, and technical aspects of publishing (print/digital).            |
| **Junior Editors** | Assists with editing, fact-checking, and administrative tasks.                          |
| **Interns**        | Supports various departments, gains experience, and assists with research or writing.  |
