Here is the way I was taught: take the first measure or phrase if it crosses a measure ( or two) , play through it five times. Take the next measure or phrase and do the same. Then back to the first for four times. 
Then the second phrase for four times. Then 3x then 2x. Ok now you are down to one phrase. 
Now continue playing both phrases one after the other for five times. 
Then you will have that much memorized and can play it without the music. Go through the whole piece like this. 
It’s called the 54321 method. It works. Sounds complicated but it is not. 
 Then when you’ve gone through the whole piece, play through five times without the music ( or with if needed). A piece like Jesu 2-3 pages at most with repeats might take a month at most. Then you should be able to sit in a dark room and play it without mistakes.
