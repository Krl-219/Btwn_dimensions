## 🧭 Explanation in My Own Words
- [ ] Insight
**Ingredients**

1. 400 g water
    
2. 800 g sucrose (table sugar)
    
3. 1 g cream of tartar
    

**Directions**

1. Add 200 grams water, 400 grams sugar, and cream of tartar to a pressure cooker and cook at 15 psi for 30 minutes to invert the sucrose.
    
2. Manually release pressure when complete, and pour into a heavy bottom, wide saucepan.
    
3. Cook on high until it reaches **338°F/170°C**.
    
4. While that is going, add remaining water and sugar (400 grams sugar and 200 grams water) to a small sauce pan. Heat until sugar melts and syrup becomes clear (simple syrup), reduce to simmer while the first syrup finishes.
    
5. Once the first syrup reaches **338°F/170°C**, remove from heat, and immediately add simple syrup. Stir for a couple minutes to cool and combine.
    
6. Once cooled around **212°F/100°C**, pour into a ball jar (before it gets too thick, but cool enough not to damage your container).
