Harriet Fitch Little's "Chicken and Brothy Peas" recipe from FT Magazine:

Ingredients:

Bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
Salt and pepper
1 finely chopped shallot (or onion)
1 clove garlic, minced
Splash of dry white wine
~500ml good quality chicken stock
Handful of fresh or frozen peas
Knob of butter
Fresh herbs (mint, parsley, dill)
Method:

Season chicken thighs with salt and pepper

Sear skin-side down in a large skillet or Dutch oven until crisp and golden, then remove and set aside

In the same pan, sauté shallot and garlic until softened

Deglaze with white wine

Add chicken stock and peas, bring to a gentle boil

Return chicken to pan, skin-side up

Simmer gently for 20-25 minutes until chicken is cooked through and peas are tender

Finish by whisking in butter and chopped fresh herbs

Adjust seasoning and serve with crusty bread, rice, or potatoes

It's a light, one-pan French-style dish with a brothy sauce. The key is getting that crispy skin on the chicken while keeping the peas tender in the herb-infused broth.
