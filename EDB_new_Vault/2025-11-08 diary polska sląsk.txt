#diary #polska #sląsk
the sense of humour here in Małopolska here is different. It’s the conservative fear of breaking rank, taking a risk. The conformity does not make for the funniest jokes. 

Why are they so desperate to conform in the day to day, checking over their shoulder before delivering a punchline.
 
Even people not from here seen to join the cult.

My parents behave this way too. What is so sacrosanct? It freezes dries some of life’s better moments. It took my psychotherapist, Peter, to say, “where is the harm in simply playing with ideas?”

Yet another clever Peter.
