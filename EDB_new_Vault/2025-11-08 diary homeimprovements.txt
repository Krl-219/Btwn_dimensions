#diary #homeimprovements 

Enjoying our new kitchen could be about as mundane as bourgeois pleasure gets. Part of admiring this material thing is the craftsmanship, particularly the tiling, which is subtle and reminds me of Paweł and his natural friendliness. I’m now around the stage of forgetting the dull, irritating parts of the experience.

It’s difficult to say that the prevailing mood in Sląsk is nicer, without implying that most people in Małopolska are a bit mean.
